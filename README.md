# ☕ Starbucks Brewing Insights | Interactive Power BI Dashboard

**Brewing Insights. Fueling Connections.**

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes Starbucks beverage nutritional data to uncover key insights on calories, sugar, caffeine content, and category-wise performance. Designed with a clean, branded UI to help stakeholders make informed decisions around product nutrition and customer preferences.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

This project transforms raw beverage nutritional data into an interactive and visually appealing Power BI dashboard. It helps stakeholders quickly understand:

- Average nutritional values across beverages
- Category-wise calorie and caffeine distribution
- Top high-caffeine drinks
- Global presence of different beverage types

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

| Tool                  | Purpose                              |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Power BI Desktop**  | Dashboard design & visualization     |
| **Power Query**       | Data cleaning & transformation       |
| **DAX**               | KPIs, measures & calculated columns  |
| **Excel / CSV**       | Source data handling                 |

---

## ✨ Features & Highlights

- **4 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
  - Total Beverages
  - Average Sugar
  - Average Calories
  - Average Caffeine

- **Interactive Filters**
  - Protein Range slider
  - Beverage Prep dropdown

- **Visualizations**
  - Average Calories by Beverage Category (Area Chart)
  - Starbucks Global Presence by Category (Bar Chart)
  - Beverage Category Distribution (Donut Chart)
  - Average Caffeine by Category (Horizontal Bar Chart)
  - Top 5 Highest Caffeine Beverages (with product images)

- Modern Starbucks-branded design with clean and professional layout

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Smoothies and Frappuccinos have the highest average calorie content, while plain Coffee has the lowest.
- Brewed Coffee (410 mg) and Caffè Americano (300 mg) are the top caffeine-rich beverages.
- Coffee category shows the strongest global presence, followed by Classic Espresso drinks.
- Average sugar across the analyzed beverages is approximately 81g, and average caffeine is around 33 mg.
- Significant variation exists in nutritional values across beverage categories, highlighting opportunities for healthier alternatives.

---

## 💼 Business Impact & Problem Solved

**Problem:**  
Starbucks offers a wide range of beverages with varying nutritional profiles. Without clear visualization, it becomes difficult for decision-makers to quickly understand calorie, sugar, and caffeine distribution across categories and identify high-impact products.

**Solution & Impact:**  
This dashboard provides a single-view interactive analysis that enables:

- Quick identification of high-calorie and high-caffeine drinks
- Better understanding of category-wise nutritional trends
- Data-backed decisions for menu optimization and healthier product promotion
- Improved visibility into global product presence and performance
